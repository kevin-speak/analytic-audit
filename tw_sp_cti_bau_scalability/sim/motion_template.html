Do the checks predict efficiency and spend?measured forward from each ad's check-in

For each ad that reached the check-in day: its metric that day vs what happened after it. "Can it spend" = rank correlation (ρ) with spend after the check-in. "Efficiency" = ρ with LTV/CAC earned after the check-in (ads with ≥ $200 later spend). Pass/fail columns compare the spend-weighted later LTV/CAC and median later spend of ads the gate passed vs failed. Grey = not significant (p ≥ 0.10). SP2 contains a spend-velocity term (SI), so part of its link to later spend is built in.

Scoreboardwinner = lifetime LTV/CAC ≥ 0.85 and spend ≥ $1k (testing) / $5k (winning, scaling) · ads under 14 days old excluded

Precision = share of ads the gate passes that finished as winners (base rate in brackets). Recall = share of winners the gate passes. "Day 7" = state at each ad's 7th delivery day. Pause policy = pause an ad after 3 consecutive failing check-ins: spend it would have saved on non-winners, versus spend winners would have lost. LTV/CAC column = final lifetime LTV/CAC of the group.

Gates, re-scored daily with cumulative data: SP ≥ 2.0 with ≥ 10 installs (Option B, benchmarked against the same campaign group's placements to date). CTI = installs ÷ link clicks ≥ 1% with ≥ 300 link clicks. SP2 = EI × SI ≥ 2 with EI ≥ 1 and ≥ 10 installs, where EI = CPI target $15.32 ÷ CPI and SI = spend per active day ÷ the median live ad in the group that day. LTV/CAC = SSOT modelled LTV by attribution date (scaled up 9–30% per group and month for LTV that SSOT could not tie to an ad) ÷ Meta spend. Data: BigQuery meta_ads_creative_report_funnel and marketing_attribution_aggregate_attribution_date_cohort, plus Meta Ads link clicks.
