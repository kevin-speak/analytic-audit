SP2 scored against LTV/CAC, the old SP and IPM for every Speak ZH app ad

Every iOS app ad with spend in the Speak ZH account between ( ads, ) is scored on that window's data alone with SP2 = Efficiency Index × Scale Index, the old Scalability Predictor (Option B), and installs per mille, then read against its simulated LTV/CAC. Each ad's LTV/CAC comes from Monte Carlo draws over its SSOT trial starts, trial-to-paid rate, plan mix and 36-month LTV, so every correlation, hit rate and lift below carries a 90% interval.

4 · Gate comparison

What each winner rule actually selects

Point columns use each ad's median LTV/CAC; interval columns re-evaluate the gate in every draw. Hit rate = share of gated ads at or above 0.8×. Recall = share of the spend that ends on target which the gate captures. Lift = spend-weighted LTV/CAC of gated ads over the rest.
