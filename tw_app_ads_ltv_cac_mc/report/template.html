Do creative signals predict LTV/CAC? A Monte Carlo read of 243 app ads

Each ad's LTV/CAC is simulated 4,000 times from its SSOT trial starts, an uncertain trial-to-paid rate, Meta-observed purchases, and the plan mix of its trialists. The same draws re-score the Scalability Predictor (SP), installs per mille (IPM) and trials per mille impressions (TPI), so every correlation and every winner-vs-rest lift below carries a 90% interval instead of a point.
